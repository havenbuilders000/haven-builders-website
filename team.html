Meet the Founder

Prince Varghese

Founder & Managing Director

Every home we build carries a family’s trust, and every structure carries our name. That is a responsibility we take seriously on every site, every day.

At Haven Builders, I want every client to feel confident from the first conversation to the day we hand over the keys. That means honest estimates, clear communication, and no shortcuts on materials or workmanship. Our team plans carefully, builds with discipline, and stays accountable at every milestone.

Whether you are building your first home, a villa, or a commercial space, we will treat it with the same care we would give our own. I look forward to building your vision with you.
